# Snaglist v0.6.0

Released 19 November 2026.

## Changed

- **Mica, the design system's new default.** When a page has no design tokens of its own, Snaglist uses Mica's neutral
  greys with its Blue accent. Its buttons, fields, banner and dialog have 6px corners. A page with its own `--radius`
  keeps it.

## Your settings

Nothing to change. Pages that set their own design tokens stay as they were. To go back, load
`snaglist.js` from the v0.5.0 tag.
